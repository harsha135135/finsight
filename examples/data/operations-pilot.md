# Fictional support operations pilot

This is synthetic demonstration evidence, not an account of a real organization.

Acme Support ran a four-week fictional trial of a searchable internal knowledge
base. Twelve participants used the prototype to locate approved support articles.
The team collected qualitative feedback, but did not record a controlled baseline
or independently measured resolution time.

Participants said the most useful features were clear article ownership and
visible revision dates. The trial owner reported that duplicate articles and
missing escalation instructions made some searches harder.

Proposed next steps in this example are to assign article owners, review stale
content, define an escalation path, and measure search success in a larger trial.
The evidence does not establish cost savings, causality, or deployment readiness.
